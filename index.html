Online Safety Tip

Be careful with anyone promising guaranteed profits or a job in exchange for an upfront payment. Verify the organisation independently before sending money or personal information.

Build Your Digital Skills

Start with simple tasks: create a strong password, learn to use email, check whether a website is trustworthy, and practise searching for reliable information online.

Protect Your Phone and Accounts

Use a screen lock, keep your phone updated, never share verification codes, and avoid clicking suspicious messages. If you think an account has been compromised, change its password using the official website or application.
